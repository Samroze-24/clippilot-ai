Long video in. Short vertical clips out. Local-first — cloud features only when you configure them.

Upload a video

MP4 / WebM / MOV / MKV, up to 500 MB. Files stay on your machine.

Or ingest a public URL

YouTube, Twitch, Kick or a direct MP4 link (public videos only, no live streams).

YouTube sometimes blocks downloads ("Requested format is not available"). Tick the box to let yt-dlp use your logged-in browser session — close the browser first if it fails. Or upload a cookies.txt (Netscape format) instead. Cookies are deleted right after the download.

Or import a Twitch channel's VODs

Needs TWITCH_CLIENT_ID + TWITCH_CLIENT_SECRET in .env (see README). Live streams are not imported — only finished VODs.
